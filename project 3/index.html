Movie Review

Dhurandhar is an upcoming Hindi action-thriller directed by Aditya Dhar. The film features Ranveer Singh in the lead role, supported by an ensemble cast including Sanjay Dutt, R. Madhavan, Akshaye Khanna, and Arjun Rampal. Inspired by events from the world of intelligence and covert operations, the movie follows a determined operative on a high-stakes mission involving espionage, patriotism, and dangerous conspiracies. Promising intense action, emotional depth, and gripping storytelling, Dhurandhar is one of the most anticipated Bollywood releases.

Movie Rating:⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐(9/10)
