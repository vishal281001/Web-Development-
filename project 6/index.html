Best Buttermilk PanCake!


Servings:6

Total Time: 20 minutes


Ingredients


  • 2 cups all-purpose flours
  • 3 tablespoon sugar
  • 3 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoon melted butter

Instructions:

  1. In alarge bowl,whisk together the flour,sugar, baking pawder and salt.

  2. In a seperate bowl,whisk together the milk, egg and melted butter.

  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Afew lumps are fine.

  4. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and pour 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake.Cook until bubbles form on the surface then flip and cook until golden brown.
